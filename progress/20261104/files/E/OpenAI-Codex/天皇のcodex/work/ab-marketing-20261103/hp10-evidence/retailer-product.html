Product Description

The 4A Sticky Memo Cube (2 1/2 Inches) features ~500 bubble-patterned, self-stick, recyclable sheets, ideal for quick notes and brainstorming.

The 4A Sticky Memo Cube, 2 1/2 Inches, stands out with its distinctive bubble pattern printed on all four sides. This self-stick notes cube offers approximately 500 sheets per pack, making it a stylish and practical choice for capturing quick messages and ideas.

Key Features:

  • Unique Bubble Pattern: Adds a playful and eye-catching design to your workspace, making your notes more special.
  • Smooth Edge Cutting: Each sheet features smoothly cut edges for a neat and premium feel.
  • Generous Quantity: With approximately 500 sheets per cube, you’ll have an ample supply for all your note-taking needs.
  • Recyclable Material: These 4A sticky notes are made from recyclable paper, supporting environmental responsibility.
  • Advanced Adhesive Technology: Developed to stick securely to most smooth surfaces, remove cleanly without residue, and allow for repositioning.

4A has been a dedicated manufacturer of sticky notes since 2000, investing seven years into research and development to perfect their adhesive technology. Since 2007, 4A has served as an OEM supplier for numerous renowned brands, solidifying its reputation for high-quality, reliable products that stick longer and remove cleanly without curving or leaving marks.

Ideal for:

  • Students and educators for organizing notes and highlighting important information.
  • Office professionals needing quick reminders and efficient task management.
  • Creative individuals and teams for capturing ideas during brainstorming sessions.
  • Anyone seeking a reliable and uniquely designed sticky memo cube for daily use.
  • Eco-conscious consumers looking for recyclable stationery options.
